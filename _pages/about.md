I'm an MSc Data Science & AI student at TU Delft, specialising in Advanced Machine Learning and Probabilistic Decision Making. Alongside my studies, I'm Co-founder & CTO of **Quantum Mind B.V.**, a TU Delft spin-off building hardware-aware AI for quantum computing. I'm interested in core AI theory that leads to real solutions for important problems.

I'm also a student researcher at the [Infini Data Lab (WIS)](https://www.tudelft.nl), working on AI for quantum simulation and error correction. This work has led to three publications: **QEC Model Zoo** (NeurIPS 2026), **InferQ** (SIGMOD 2027) and **QEC lake** (VLDB 2026). See the [publications](/publications/) page for details.

Outside of tech, I'm into chess (FIDE 1773), cricket (Dutch National U16 & U18 teams), ancient history, and science fiction.

Feel free to reach out if anything here resonates — always happy to connect.
